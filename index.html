Bloomflow

A little water. A little wonder.

An offline single-player puzzle from Minicto Inc. Rotate water channels to connect every flower across 60 levels in three gardens. Take your time: there are no timers, paid hints or lives.

The Android game has no accounts, advertising, in-app purchases or cloud saves. Progress stays on your device.
