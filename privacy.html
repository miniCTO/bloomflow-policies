Bloomflow Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

1. Who operates Bloomflow

Bloomflow is operated by Minicto Inc (miniCTO). Contact us at suin2032932@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

2. Offline gameplay and local data

Bloomflow is an offline single-player puzzle game. You do not need an account. The Android app stores completed levels, best turn counts, your current puzzle, undo history, tutorial status and sound, music, haptics and motion preferences on your device. It keeps a previous local save snapshot to recover from a damaged save. This gameplay data is not sent to Minicto Inc or to an analytics service.

3. Data we do not collect through the game

The Android game does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, remote crash reporting, in-app purchases, social features or cloud saves. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or advertising identifier. Its illustrations, fonts, levels and audio are bundled with the app. It does not require an Internet connection for gameplay.

4. Contacting support

If you choose Contact support, the app opens your email application with our support address. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself. We then receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use those details only to answer your request, troubleshoot the issue and maintain a record of support correspondence. Our email provider processes those messages to provide email services. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

5. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Settings > Reset progress removes level progress and the active puzzle while keeping preferences. Settings > Delete local data clears progress and restores default preferences; the app then stores only its fresh default state. Both actions also remove previous progress recovery snapshots. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for app-managed data. We cannot recover erased offline progress.

Support messages are normally deleted within 90 days after the request is resolved. You may email suin2032932@gmail.com to ask for deletion of support correspondence. We will process a verified request within 15 calendar days. We may retain limited records if required by law or necessary to address an unresolved security or legal matter, and will explain any applicable exception. Our email provider's backup retention may follow its own policies.

6. Sharing and security

We do not sell personal data or share gameplay data for advertising. We may provide support correspondence to service providers who process it on our behalf, or disclose it when legally required. Local saves use the application's private device storage. Keep your device secure. No method of storage or communication is absolutely secure.

7. Younger users

Bloomflow is designed as a general-interest puzzle for teenagers and adults, not specifically for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support mailbox, contact us so we can remove it.

8. External services and this website

Opening support email or an external policy website uses the service you choose, which has its own privacy practices. The Android game does not embed an advertising or tracking website. A public website hosting this policy may receive technical web request information according to that hosting provider's policies; that is separate from offline Android gameplay.

9. Your choices and changes

You can play without creating an account, disable optional audio and haptics, and delete local data at any time. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information held in support correspondence. Contact us to exercise those rights. If our practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date, and provide additional notice where required.
