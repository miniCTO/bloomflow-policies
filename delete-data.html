Delete Bloomflow data

Bloomflow has no account system or server-side gameplay records. You do not need to submit an account deletion request.

On your device

Open Bloomflow → Settings → Delete local data → confirm. This clears level progress, the active board and undo history, and restores default preferences. Previous recovery snapshots are also removed. The game then starts with a fresh default state.

Alternatively, use Android Settings → Apps → Bloomflow → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Menu names vary by device. Cleared offline progress cannot be recovered by Minicto Inc.

After uninstalling

No gameplay deletion request is needed: we do not receive your local game data. If you previously emailed support and want that correspondence deleted, email suin2032932@gmail.com from the address you used. Do not send passwords or identity documents.
